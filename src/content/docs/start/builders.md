You don't need to know how to code. You describe what you want, and Sprout does
the building, one small task at a time.

## 1. Describe your idea

On the home page, answer **What do you want to build?** in your own words — for
example, *"A one-page site for my plant shop with opening hours and a contact
form."* Then choose **Start**.

Sprout, the agent beside every page, takes the conversation from there: it asks
a few short questions (who it's for, what it should include) and agrees a plan
with you, while the plan and a first look take shape on the page. Nothing is
built until you're happy with the plan.

## 2. Watch it come together

When you start building, the plan becomes a list of **tasks**. They run one at
a time, on machines in the cloud — you can close the tab, and the work carries
on.

Open your project to follow along. Each task shows:

- **You asked** — what the task set out to do;
- **What happened** — a plain-words summary of the result;
- **What changed** — the files it touched;
- whether the change was **added to your project**.

Every task is reviewed before its change is added to your project. If a task
stops to ask you something, answer it in the project and the task carries on.

## 3. See it running

Choose **Preview** in your project to see your app as it is right now. To change
something, describe the change under **What should change?** and choose
**Start** — it becomes the next task.

## 4. Put it online

Choose **Put it online** to give your app its own address and share it. Previews
are included on every plan, with a monthly allowance of preview requests on the
Free plan; going live is part of the paid plans. See
[Put your app online](/guides/put-it-online/).

## What it costs

Work on our machines and our models uses **credits**. The Free plan has a hard
cap, so it never charges you. See [Credits](/concepts/credits/).
