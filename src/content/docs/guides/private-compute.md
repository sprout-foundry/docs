**Private compute** is an add-on for the Pro plan. With it on:

- managed model calls go **only to zero-data-retention models**, and
- your workspaces and tasks run **only on Sprout's own machines**, never on
  third-party cloud compute.

It also gives your work priority on our machines.

## Turn it on

Open **Account settings → Private compute** and add it to your plan.

## Good to know

- Calls made with [your own model keys](/guides/bring-your-own-keys/) go to
  your provider under your agreement with them.
- See [Security and data](/security/overview/) for what Sprout records and where
  data is hosted.
