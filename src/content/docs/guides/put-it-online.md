Every project gets its own address the first time it's built. Open your project
and choose **Put it online**.

## Previews

A **preview** shows the current version of your app at a link you can share.
Anyone with the link can open it, and search engines skip it. Previews are free;
preview links expire after a while, and you can make a new one at any time.

## Make it live

Choose **Make it live** to put the current version online for everyone at your
app's address. Going live is part of the paid plans; each paid plan includes a
monthly allowance of requests to your live apps, shared by all of them, and
traffic beyond it uses credits. See [Credits](/concepts/credits/) and the
[pricing page](/pricing/).

## Go back to an earlier version

The **Put it online** page lists what you've put online. Choose **Go back** on
an earlier version to make it the live one again.

## Your own hosting account (optional)

Under **Use your own hosting account**, you can put the app online on a hosting
account of your own instead.

## If an app is paused

If your account runs out of credits, live apps pause after a grace period and
come back within minutes once you add credits or pick a plan.
